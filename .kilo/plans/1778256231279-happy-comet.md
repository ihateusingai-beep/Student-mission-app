# 島民小英雄 - Stats & Progress Tracking Enhancement Plan

## Executive Summary

Currently, stats and progress data exist but are fragmented: weekly summaries are text-only alerts, ability progress is buried in components, and the most insightful "Hero Review" feature is hidden behind the admin PIN. This plan creates a unified, visually engaging **Stats Dashboard** that makes progress tangible and motivating.

---

## 1. New Stats Dashboard Screen

### 1.1 Overview
- **New file**: `src/screens/StatsScreen.tsx`
- **Tab access**: Available from main tab bar (alongside Home, Tasks, Rewards, Badges)
- **Purpose**: Central hub for all statistics and progress visualization

### 1.2 Dashboard Sections

#### A. Quick Stats Header
- Total points earned (all time)
- Current streak days
- Tasks completed (today / week / all time)
- Badges unlocked count

#### B. Weekly Progress Card
- Circular progress ring showing weekly completion rate
- Bar chart for daily completion (Mon-Sun)
- Comparison to previous week (% improvement)

#### C. Ability Levels Panel
- Same 2x2 grid as `AbilityProgress.tsx` but with level-up animations
- Shows current level and XP progress bar to next level
- Color-coded by ability type

#### D. Category Breakdown
- Pie chart or horizontal bar chart showing task distribution by category
- Top 3 categories highlighted
- Motivational message based on balance

#### E. Recent Achievements
- Horizontal scroll of recently earned badges/certificates
- Streak milestones (7 days, 30 days)
- Special achievements

### 1.3 Visual Components Needed
- `WeeklyChart.tsx` - Bar chart component for daily/weekly trends
- `CategoryPieChart.tsx` - Simple pie/donut chart for category breakdown
- `StatCard.tsx` - Reusable card for quick stat displays

## 2. Enhanced Weekly Review Screen

### 2.1 Problem
Current `formatHeroReviewText()` in `WeeklyReview.ts` produces plain text alerts. Users can't see visual charts or interact with the data.

### 2.2 Solution
- **New file**: `src/screens/WeeklyReviewScreen.tsx`
- **Accessible from**: Stats Dashboard → "View Full Report" button
- **Design**: Full-screen visual report with the following sections:

#### A. Hero Review Header
- Week number and date range
- Overall grade/score (A-F or stars)
- Animated confetti for high performers (>80%)

#### B. Completion Statistics
- Large circular progress ring (tasks completed vs target)
- Daily breakdown bar chart
- Category pie chart

#### C. Ability Progression
- 4 ability bars showing level and XP
- Week-over-week change indicators (↑↓→)
- Suggested focus areas

#### D. Self-Assessment Summary
- 3 radial gauges for Focus/Resilience/Time Management
- Comparison to previous week

#### E. Strengths & Improvements
- Card-based list with icons
- Auto-generated based on data patterns

#### F. Next Week Goals
- 3 recommended goals with checkboxes
- Progress tracking for ongoing goals

## 7. UI/UX Design Guidelines

### 7.1 Color Scheme
- Primary: `#3b82f6` (Blue - progress/achievements)
- Success: `#22c55e` (Green - completion)
- Warning: `#f59e0b` (Amber - streaks)
- Purple: `#8b5cf6` (Abilities/special)

### 7.2 Layout
- StatsScreen: Scrollable dashboard with card sections
- WeeklyReviewScreen: Full-screen modal with scroll
- Charts: Clean, minimal, with clear labels
- Animations: Subtle scale/fade on mount, confetti on achievements

### 7.3 Typography
- Stats values: Large bold numbers
- Labels: Medium weight, secondary color
- Descriptions: Regular weight, muted color
